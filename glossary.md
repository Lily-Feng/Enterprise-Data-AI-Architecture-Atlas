# Glossary

**Capability** — A durable outcome a platform enables, independent of any one product.

**Decision intelligence** — The combination of data, models, context, workflows, and feedback used to improve decisions.

**Digital twin** — A maintained digital representation of a real-world entity or system, often including state and behavior.

**Knowledge graph** — Entities and relationships represented as a graph, typically with explicit semantics and graph-oriented query or inference.

**Lakehouse** — An architecture that combines data-lake economics and openness with data-warehouse management and analytics features.

**Ontology** — A governed operational model of business objects, relationships, properties, policies, and actions. See [Ontology](capabilities/ontology.md).

**Operational analytics** — Analytics embedded in or close to a business workflow, usually with low-latency data and an action loop.

**Reverse ETL** — Moving curated warehouse or lakehouse data into operational applications.

**Semantic layer** — Shared definitions for business measures, dimensions, and relationships used by analytics tools.

**Serving layer** — Systems optimized to expose prepared data to applications, APIs, dashboards, or models.

**System of action** — A system that turns context and decisions into governed changes in operational systems.

**System of record** — The authoritative source for a class of business data.

**Vector search** — Retrieval based on similarity between learned numerical representations.

Definitions here are deliberately practical. Individual pages should explain narrower or competing definitions when they matter.
