How to use this

Work in Practice mode by domain first, reading every explanation including the ones you got right — the explanation says why the distractors lose, which is the actual skill being tested. Move to Full exam only once you are reading explanations and finding nothing new.

The pass mark is 720 / 1000. The scaled score shown here is an estimate from your raw percentage — the real exam's scale comes from a standard-setting study, so treat a borderline result here as not yet ready.

Progress is saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the tab and resume. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
