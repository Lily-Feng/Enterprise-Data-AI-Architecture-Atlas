# Review status

**This fixture has not had independent domain review, and until it does it is
not fit to be evaluation ground truth.**

That distinction matters more here than it looks. Every claim below is now bound
to quoted evidence from a fetched government page, and that machinery is worth
what it cost — it blocks a fee that contradicts its own citation. What it cannot
do is notice that a correctly quoted page is being *applied* to the wrong
situation, or that an obligation described accurately in 2022 was changed in
2024.

Two errors of exactly that kind were found by review, not by the gate:

| Was | Is |
|---|---|
| "Texas LLCs owe an annual franchise tax report even in years when no tax is due" | For report years 2024 and later the separate No Tax Due Report was discontinued. An entity at or below the threshold files a Public Information Report or Ownership Report. |
| A missed Form 2553 window "costs a full year" | The IRS documents conditional relief for late elections. The window still matters; the consequence is not automatic. |

A third: the start-up cost election cited Publication 535, which the IRS
discontinued after its 2022 revision. It now rests on Publication 583, and
Publication 535's own page is retained as the evidence that it is gone.

## What review still needs to cover

- The Texas franchise tax threshold and which report applies, per report year
- Whether T002's claim about banks requiring an operating agreement is
  generally true or merely common
- Form 205's stated fee against the form instructions, not only the fee schedule
- Whether the Solo 401(k) establishment deadline is stated correctly for each
  plan type, which Publication 560 treats separately

  recognise

## Standing rule

A claim that cannot be evidenced from a fetched tier-1 page does not ship. The
start-up cost deadline was removed under this rule rather than restated from
memory, and the two service-cost figures were removed earlier for the same
reason.
